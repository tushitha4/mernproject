Welcome to The Golden Whisk. We believe the magic is in the ingredients. Flour, water, and time. Every loaf is handcrafted and baked in our stone hearth oven to ensure a perfect crust and airy crumb. Stop by for your daily bread or stay for coffee and a treat.

Our croissants take days to prepare. We use high-fat European-style butter and create hundreds of slow-fermented flaky layers. It is a labor of love that results in a taste that transports you straight to Paris. Pairs perfectly with our locally roasted espresso.
